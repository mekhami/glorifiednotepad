yeah that's the summary, really.

i need to be a better person.

i need to do better for my kids. they deserve the kind of childhood that they look back on fondly all the time. i need to be less frustrated, less irritated, less angry. more accepting, more attention, more affection. i try my best.

but trying your best, contrary to popular belief, is not enough. you need to do what you set out to do. failing hurts people. it hurts yourself and others and hurting yourself hurts others.

i need to achieve my goals too. if i don't write i will just regret it all the time. just start writing. write until you die. if you write enough some percentage of it will be good. shakespeare typewriter monkeys. there's no reason not to, you have nothing to lose.

focusing is hard. not focusing is worse. everything is a distraction and "they" are attacking you with distraction all day every day. your attention is money they are stealing from you. grand theft auto, taking your brain for a joyride and its all perfectly legal.

my focus should be on my family. and also on me. i deserve some of my attention too. i have always given myself most of my attention and justified it. is it fair? is it right? who's to say

friends are nice, but will they be here when i'm an asshole? my kids will be.

i have to be more patient

i can be funny sometimes. my kids think i'm funny. my kids worship me and it's stupid because on the whole i'm pretty selfish. i justify being selfish by saying mortality makes it okay. i'm gonna die one day, why would i not care about myself when there's so little time. is that fair? i just don't know but maybe i can do it and still be a good person?

i want to be an example to my kids of someone who does things, who makes things, who isn't jaeger piloted by the asshole class.
