System tensions.

In HMTW, there's tension between the phases - Crawl, Challenge, Camp, City.

City pressures the wallet which drives players to the crawl.

Crawl and Challenge pressure health, status, and inventory - which drives players to the camp.

Camp pressures resources that can only be acquired in the city, which drives players eventully to the city.
